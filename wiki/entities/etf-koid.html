KOID — KraneShares Global Humanoid Robotics and Physical AI Index ETF

largest liquid vehicle for the humanoid-robotics theme — an index fund tracking the MerQube
Global Humanoid Robotics and Physical AI Index across OEMs and, mostly, their supply chain.

Holdings character

An equal-weight-ish ecosystem basket rather than a pure OEM bet. That lowers single-name blow-up
risk and makes it the more investable of the two humanoid ETFs, but it also dilutes purity: a
good year for humanoids shows up only partly in KOID, while a bad year for connectors or rare
earths shows up fully. Roughly a third of assets sit in China/HK-listed names — export-control
and sentiment risk travels with it. (inference)
