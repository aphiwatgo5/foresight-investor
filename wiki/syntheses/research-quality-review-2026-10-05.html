Research Quality Review — Self-Audit of the 100x-Hunt Chain

Bottom line: Operator asked: "evaluate the quality of your research; if too bad, improve it."
Honest verdict on round 1: C+ overall — process discipline was good, but the financial tables in
the 10 memos were materially wrong or thin on 5 of 10 names, validation leaned bullish, and the
"100x from today" framing understated how much this basket had already re-rated. This document grades
each layer, lists every error found, and records the fixes applied (round-2 memos now carry real
statements + red-flag blocks). Post-fix grade: B+; the residual gaps are named below.

Errors found in round-1 memos (all corrected in round 2)

  1. IOVA: FY2025 revenue stated "~$140M (est.)" — actual $263.5M (yfinance). 2026 guide = +57%, not +180%.
  2. OABI: presented as an inflecting royalty platform — actuals show a 3-year DECLINING revenue base ($59.1M '22 → $18.7M '25), SBC at 84% of revenue, FY25 cash $25.5M (~8 months at burn). Rating cut BUY → ACCUMULATE-small (0.25%).
  3. GRAL: asserted "cash-rich via spin (est.)" — actual FY2025 opCF −$299M vs $249.7M cash ≈ 10 months runway → dilution risk INTO the FDA catalyst; GM was −42.5% (allocation-heavy). Position sized 0.4→0.3%.
  4. ATEX: FY2026 revenue estimated "~$11-12M" — actual $6.5M, and the +$90.6M NI is a one-off license-sale gain; the model is a lumpy spectrum land-bank (net cash ~$94M floor), not a growth-revenue line.
  5. SEZL/DAVE/GCT scale errors: SEZL's FY2025 revenue is $450.3M (bigger than implied) with $133M NI and +$210M opCF — reinforces the thesis; GCT growth corrected 15-20% → +11% actual.
  6. Framing error (the big one): momentum audit shows IOVA +508%, NLST +444%, ATEX +274%, OABI +199%, GRAL +119%, ABCL +133% over 12m — six of ten names already re-rated. This is an early-inflection basket, not a pre-rise one; only SEZL (−43% off high, short 26%) and HTFL (+31%/12m) still resemble the framework's P5 mark. Entries must be staged/limit-based accordingly.

Verdict for the operator
