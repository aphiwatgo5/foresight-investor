Thai value & dividend income play — SET income sleeve (2026-09-26)

Bottom line: For the operator's own book. A THB income sleeve of 3–5% of the book, built from the highest-yield, cheapest quartile of the Thai large caps — a deliberate income-and-value position that diversifies the USD/AI-capex-heavy core. The screen (live yfinance 2026-09-26, THB, SET): PTTEP 6.2% yield / 8.5× fwd PE · LH 6.6% / 10.4× · PTT 4.9% / 11.4× · ADVANC 5.0% / 18.0× · KBANK 4.8% / 11.1×. Honest tension: our FFM (momentum-tilted in a bull regime) rates all of them Hold except GULF/LH (Sell) — the FFM is not an income model; this sleeve is justified by yield + valuation + currency diversification, not by factor momentum, and it must be sized accordingly (income bucket, never offense).

The sleeve (suggested structure, own book)

  • Core (income bucket, 3–5% total): PTTEP 1.5% · ADVANC 1.0% · KBANK 1.0% · PTT 0.5–1.0% (holdover). Blended yield ≈ 5.2–5.5% in THB, blended fwd PE ≈ 10–11× — roughly half the S&P's multiple with double its yield, in the operator's home currency.
  • Optional satellite: LH +0.5% for the 6.6% yield — but growth F means it's a yield-trap candidate; only with the invalidation below.
  • Skip: SCC, GULF (failed the screen), DELTA/AOT (growth/tourism names, not income).
  • Vehicle note: THD (Thailand ETF) yields ~2–3% only — direct SET names are the right income vehicle; ETF for the liquid sleeve if the operator wants frictions low.

Why now (and why small)

  1. Valuation gap: the book's offense is USD AI-capex at record multiples; this sleeve buys 8–11× energy/bank/telecom cash flows in THB — a real diversifier (different currency, sector, and factor).
  2. Rate reality cuts both ways: US 10Y at 5.19% raises the hurdle — a 6.2% PTTEP yield clears it; ADVANC's 5.0% barely does; nothing below ~4.5% qualifies. Thai policy rates trail the US, so THB income is not competing with T-bills for a THB-based operator.
  3. Oil exposure note: PTTEP+PTT make the sleeve ~60% energy-correlated — accept it (that's the value) or trim PTTEP if the book is already oil-heavy via DBC/ECO (ours is — so PTTEP at 1.5%, not more).
  4. FFM honesty: every name is Hold-or-worse on momentum-regime factors. This sleeve is a factor bet against the momentum regime — income + value + EM vs US growth. If the AI melt-up continues, this sleeve lags; that is the explicit trade-off.

Risks / invalidation

  • PTTEP: Brent <$70 sustained → earnings yield compresses; invalidation: div cut or Brent <$65 for 2 quarters.
  • ADVANC: Thai telecom price war / spectrum costs; invalidation: yield <$4% on price rally or payout cut.
  • KBANK: Thai household debt + NPL cycle; invalidation: NPL ratio >4% or yield <$3.8%.
  • Currency (if USD-funded): THB appreciation >5% vs USD erodes the income in USD terms; hedge or accept.
  • Sleeve-level: if blended yield falls below US 10Y (5.19%) or SET breaks −15%, the income thesis is dead — exit to BIL.
  • Political/policy risk is a permanent SET discount — that's why the yield exists; monitor, don't time it.
