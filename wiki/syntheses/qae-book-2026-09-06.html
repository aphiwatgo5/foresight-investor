What would change this book

  • Engine A re-ranks in 21 trading days (2026-10-05). A name leaving the top quartile of
    the composite is sold regardless of P&L. Any name through its 2.5×ATR stop is sold on the
    day.
  • Engine B flips only at the monthly check; the two names to watch are DBC (a 12m return
    below QQQ's hands the leg to QQQ) and BIL (if no candidate beats T-bills, the leg goes to the
    best of IEF/TLT/GLD/BIL — today that would be BIL, since all three others are negative
    over 12m).
  • System: a −15% peak-to-trough drawdown is treated as regime change
    (strategy-tournament-2026-09-06 §6), not as an entry.

Confidence & gaps

Confidence 3/5 on the construction, 2/5 on the specific names. The system's evidence is
described honestly in strategy-tournament-2026-09-06 §5: a +0.27 Sharpe edge inside a
+1.01 luck band, a survivor-biased stock universe (mitigated by the +6.7%/yr alpha over that
universe's own equal weight), and a diversifier leg that is currently 100% in a commodity ETF
at its high. What this book does have that the 2026-08-29 book did not: a rule for every
exit, a documented reason for every weight, and nine years of the same rules applied to the
same universe with costs.
