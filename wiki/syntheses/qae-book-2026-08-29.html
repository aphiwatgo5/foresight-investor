The concentration problem, stated plainly

Even after the max-3-per-theme cap, semis + AI-infrastructure + AI-software ≈ 58% of the
fully-invested book. Against the portfolio-blueprint rule AI-capex cluster ≤ 25%, this
book breaches the cap roughly 2×. That is not an engine error — it is what a momentum
screen returns in an AI-capex bull market, and it is exactly the failure mode
quant-firm-anatomy §4 warns about: fifteen names that are one bet. Any real deployment
must either cut the cluster to the cap or accept the concentration knowingly.

6. Confidence & gaps

Confidence: low-to-moderate (2.5/5).
- The signal evidence is thin: the best factor has a Newey–West t of 1.2 over 5 years, and
with 14 factors tested a multiple-testing bar would demand ~3.0. Momentum is used because
it is one of the most-replicated effects in the literature and its sign agrees here —
not because this study proved it.
- Effective factor breadth is ~1: both surviving factors are momentum variants.
- The backtest window (~3y) is a single bull regime, and the universe is the list tracked
today (survivorship). Both flatter the result.
- The book is one trading session stale, and the fundamental overlay is 17 days stale.

What would change the read: SPY closing below its 200dma (momentum's regime support
gone) · mom_12_1 rolling IC turning negative · the AI-capex cluster cracking, which would
hit ~58% of this book simultaneously.
