Gold — 10-Year Study & Fee-Aware Timing Strategy

Bottom line: Over 2016-09→2026-09 gold returned +232% (12.7% CAGR, maxDD −25%) — and no
timing rule we tested beat buy-and-hold on CAGR this decade, because gold's bear years were shallow
(−2.1%, −3.5%, −0.4%) inside a structural supercycle driven by central-bank buying >1,000t/yr
(2022-24) + ETF re-entry (~600t in 2025) (web: WGC/Reuters/Morgan Stanley, 2026-10-06). What timing
DOES buy is drawdown control: the best overlay cuts maxDD −23%→−17% for ~3.5pts of CAGR. The
mechanical recommendation: core = buy-and-hold; risk overlay = 3m&12m momentum (or TIP real-rate
filter) checked MONTHLY (fee-efficient); never fund leverage via GDX (−46/−55% maxDD). Full engine:
scripts/gold_timing.py; raw results scripts/_hist/gold_strategy_report.json. Own-book research,
not advice.

2. Key drivers (data + web)

  1. Central-bank demand (structural, dominant 2022-26): >1,000t/yr 2022-2024 (1,082/1,037/1,045t),
    863t in 2025 — de-dollarization reserve shift; BRICS+ >50% of purchases (web: WGC/Reuters 2026-10-06).
  2. ETF flows (marginal price-setter): absent in 2024, then ~600t IN in 2025 as Western investors
    re-entered — the accelerant behind +64.5% (web: Morgan Stanley/WGC).
  3. Real rates (weakened link): historical corr −0.7 → only −0.2/−0.3 in 2024-26 (web: Wealthion);
    in OUR data TIP (real-rate bond price) explains most of the monthly variance alongside silver:
    regression gold ~ TIP+TLT+SPY+SI → TIP beta +0.68, SI +0.35, R² 0.64 — direction intact
    (falling real yields lift gold) but magnitude dominated by flows since 2022.
  4. Dollar & risk events: COVID crash (gold −9.3% in a week, liquidity squeeze) then QE surge;
    2022 rate shock chopped (−0.4% year); geopolitical shocks = episodic spikes.
  5. Momentum structure: monthly autocorrelation only +0.09 — trends exist at QUARTER/YEAR
    scale, not month scale → rules must be slow (this explains why fast filters bleed fees).

5. The strategy (mechanical, fee-aware)

Core (the return engine): 80% of the gold sleeve = buy-and-hold. This decade it beat every rule;
the supercycle drivers (CB buying, de-dollarization) are still active (trend-radar conviction 3.5).

Risk overlay (the insurance): 20% tactical, S3 3m&12m momentum checked MONTHLY (monthly ≈ weekly
performance at 1/3 the trades → at 25bps/side costs only ~0.1%/yr in fees). Enter the tactical sleeve
only when both 3m AND 12m returns >0; exit to T-bills when 12m turns negative.

Optional regime gauge (for sizing the whole sleeve): TIP > 50-day MA — the real-rate filter had
the best drawdown (−17%); use it to scale core 100%→60% when real yields surge (TIP below MA), not
to day-trade.

What NOT to do (tested, rejected): GDX/miner "leverage" — both variants halved Sharpe and
delivered −46/−55% drawdowns; fast weekly single-MA switching — 42 trades × fees with no CAGR edge;
holding silver as a separate position — ρ 0.79 means it's the same trade with more noise.

Fee mechanics: at 10-25bps/side (typical broker+spread on gold ETFs/futures), monthly-cadence
rules retain ~97% of gross; weekly cadence ~93%; daily would be fee-destroyed. Thai access adds
spread — use the lowest-TER vehicle (e.g., GLDM/IAU class vs GLD; local gold funds for THB lines).

Key structural reads from the episode:

intact through it — our drivers (§2) unchanged.

silver goes vertical (+50%/month), the complex is in blow-off (BIS warning realized).

(deleveraging binds everything); gold↔BTC +0.31. Diversification FAILED exactly when needed — in
