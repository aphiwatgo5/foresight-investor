Lesson 1: in a momentum melt-up, the engine's factor reads (AMD/MU/TSM Strong Buy) were right while its macro caution (value rotation, gold ballast, rate-trigger trims) was a consistent drag. The allocator's caps prevented the reverse error (no underweight AI), which is why the book still worked — but the memo's advice layer was too bearish-tilted for this regime. Action: memos should separate the factor view (weight it) from the macro narrative (flag it, don't trade it) when breadth is deteriorating but price confirms momentum.
