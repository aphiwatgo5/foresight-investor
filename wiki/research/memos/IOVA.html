tumor-resident T cells, expand (iCTC platform), re-infuse. Amtagvi approved for advanced melanoma
($515K list); Proleukin supports. The iCTC platform is the scale/margin lever (one-time frontier for
personalized cell therapy logistics).

4. Industry outlook

Adoptive cell therapy is graduating from blood cancers (CAR-T) to solid tumors — the frontier
oncology forgot because logistics were brutal (vein-to-vein ~34 days). TAM: melanoma first (~$1-2B),
but the prize is lung/other epithelial tumors where TIL biology travels (inference; multi-indication
readouts pending). ARK's genomics pillar ("Great Acceleration") frames biology+compute convergence.
Competitors: none commercial in TIL at Iovance's scale (T-Carta/Instil early). Reimbursement: $515K
price is CMS-blessed today, a political variable tomorrow.
