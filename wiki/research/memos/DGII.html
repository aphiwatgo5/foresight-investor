4. Industry outlook

IoT connectivity is a slow-then-lumpy adoption curve; the 2026 re-acceleration likely reflects utility/infrastructure + datacenter-adjacent demand (Opengear's out-of-band management rides DC buildouts — inference). Competitors: Sierra Wireless (acquired), Telit, Advantech — a consolidated field where Digi's niche is industrial-grade reliability + services mix.
