4. Industry outlook

Antibodies are ~half of biologic drug approvals and the industry's workhorse modality (antibody market
$400B+ by 2030, est./inference); discovery platforms that de-risk hits (transgenic animals + AI-guided
screening) monetize across EVERY partner's success — the "picks-and-shovels of biologics." The TCE/
bispecific wave (validated by AbCellera's Jazz deal) expands platform demand. Competitors: humanized-
mouse alternatives (Harbour/Kymab-legacy) — differentiation is throughput + IP estate. ARK's genomics
pillar overlaps.
