ACCUMULATE (starter). The purest regulatory moat in the sleeve: Anterix holds the only
nationwide utility-focused 900MHz broadband spectrum (FCC-expanded to 10MHz). Utilities digitizing
the grid (our conviction-4 power-grid-electrification theme, supercharged by AI-datacenter power

utility-grade churn (zero). FY2026: revenue +41%, 92 counties licensed, ~$120M cash proceeds guided.
100x-shape probability: low (<5%) — utility sales cycles (18-36m) cap the slope; this is a
duration play with a spectrum floor. 12m fair value $95-110.

2. Company overview

Anterix (Woodland Park, CO) aggregated 900MHz narrowband spectrum nationwide, got FCC blessing to
refarm to 3×3+3MHz broadband, and licenses it to investor-owned utilities for private LTE — bundled
with network design and partner ecosystem (Cisco/Ericsson-class). ~$1.65B cap.

4. Industry outlook

Grid digitization is a forced-spend wave: distribution automation, DER interconnection (solar/EV/
batteries), and wildfire/fault isolation all require low-band private networks with deterministic
latency — exactly 900MHz physics. The AI-datacenter power squeeze (our M1-M2 theme linkage) makes
utility capex LESS deferrable, not more. Competitive alternatives: 3.5GHz CBRS (coverage-poor for
rural feeders), unlicensed (reliability-poor), fiber (capex-heavy). Spectrum scarcity is structural —
the FCC cannot mint more low-band. Small addressable base (~100 IOUs) but each contract is
seven-to-nine-figure and sticky.
