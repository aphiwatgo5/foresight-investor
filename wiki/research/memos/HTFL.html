2. Company overview

HeartFlow One converts standard coronary CT into personalized 3D models + FFRct (fractional flow
reserve, computed) — replacing invasive diagnostic catheterization for a large subset. Second product
line (Plaque/Roadmap Analyzer) expands per-station revenue. IPO Aug-2025 ($364M raise, $2.2B valuation
then; now $4.3B).

4. Industry outlook

Coronary artery disease diagnostics: ~$1B FFR market growing 11.2% CAGR to $2.06B by 2030 (validated);
HeartFlow grows 40%+ inside it = share gains + category expansion (non-invasive-first pathways).
Structural tailwinds: CV imaging volumes rising; AI-diagnostics reimbursement codes maturing; the
"avoid unnecessary cath" economics strengthen as payors tighten in a 5.28%-10Y world. Competitors:
invasive FFR (Abbott/J&J disposables) defending an eroding position; no direct CT-FFR competitor at
scale (category monopoly).
