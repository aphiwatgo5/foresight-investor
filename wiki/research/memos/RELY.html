2. Company overview

Remitly is a digital-first remittance platform for immigrant corridors (US→LatAm/Asia/Philippines etc.): app-based send, local cash-pickup + bank + mobile-wallet rails, take-rate ~2.9% on ~$50B+ annualized send volume (est.). Newer layers: Remitly Circle, banking features.

4. Industry outlook

Global remittances ≈ $800-900B/yr flows (est./inference), still 60%+ offline at initiation; digital share compounding. Unit economics: transaction-scale + corridor density = the moat (Western Union's decay is the proof). Competitors: Wise (different FX model), Western Union/MoneyGram (legacy), Zelle-ish domestic rails. Watch FX volatility as a revenue line.
