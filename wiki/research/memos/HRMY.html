4. Industry outlook

Narcolepsy/IH: small patient pools (~200K US diagnosed, est.) but chronic, under-diagnosed, with rising diagnosis rates (sleep-medicine awareness + home testing). Wakix faces stimulant competition (Jazz's Xyrem franchise) but differentiates on non-controlled scheduling. The cash pile signals pipeline M&A optionality (inference).
