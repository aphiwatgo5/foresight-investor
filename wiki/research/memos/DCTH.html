1. Recommendation

ACCUMULATE (0.3%, high-variance lottery tier). Commercial-stage oncology at genuine microcap scale ($525M): revenue $2M → $37M → $85M (2023→2025) with Q2-26 printing $29.1M single-quarter (+20.6% y/y, GM 89.8%, NI positive in 2 of last 4 quarters). Net cash $90M, zero debt. The 100x-shape is real (small base × orphan-device pricing), but the offsetting cost is a +30.2%/yr dilution history (16→36M shares) — the bet is that operating profitability now funds the ramp instead of the ATM. Scorecard: 7/8 (traj 3 · prof 2 · BS 2 · disc 1); dilution is the missing deduction.

2. Company overview

Delcath markets the HEPZATO Kit (HEPATIC CHEMOSAT — percutaneous hepatic perfusion): high-dose melphalan delivered to the liver with filtration before systemic return. First FDA approval 2023 (ocular melanoma liver mets); commercial build-out 2024-26.

4. Industry outlook

Liver-directed oncology: ocular melanoma is the beachhead (~small TAM but monopoly pricing ~$85M run-rate already); expansion into HCC/hepatic mets is the optionality that 5-10×'s the TAM (inference). Competitive field: TACE/Y-90 interventional standards — CHEMOSAT differentiates on high-dose-first-pass economics. Reimbursement established via NTAP/packaged payments (fact); watch Medicare rate cycles.
