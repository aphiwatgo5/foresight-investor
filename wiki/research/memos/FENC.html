4. Industry outlook

Ménière's: ~600K US diagnosed patients (est.) with no approved pharmacologic alternative until Otividex (betahistine never FDA-approved). Chronic recurring condition = refill economics. Competitors: off-label steroids/betahistine (gray), diuretics (weak evidence); device (SonoTree etc.) early. The category is Fennec's to own for years (inference).
