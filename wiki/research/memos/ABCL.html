4. Industry outlook

AI-driven drug discovery is bifurcating: tool-sellers (compute/plates) vs outcome-owners (platforms
that keep economics). AbCellera chose the harder, more valuable path — the Jazz TCE structure (upfront
+ big milestones + royalties on GI-cancer programs) is the template. Antibody modalities keep widening
(bispecifics/TCEs are the fastest-growing class — the same wave OABI monetizes). Risks: platform
commoditization as AI tooling spreads; big pharma in-sourcing. ARK's genomics pillar is the tailwind
frame.
