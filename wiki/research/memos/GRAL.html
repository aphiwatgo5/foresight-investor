4. Industry outlook

Cancer screening economics are asymmetric to treatment: late-stage oncology costs $150B+/yr in the US
(est./inference); a test finding cancers at Stage I-II re-prices the whole chain. Competitive field:
Exact Sciences (multi-cancer efforts), Natera (different modality), startups — but no one has Galleri's
evidence base + sequencing pedigree. The MCED category's 2030s TAM: tens of millions of tests/yr
globally (inference). Political/clinical risk: overdiagnosis debate; specificity discipline as volume
scales.
