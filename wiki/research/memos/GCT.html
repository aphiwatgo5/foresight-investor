4. Industry outlook

US furniture/large-item B2B commerce is a ~$100B+ fragmented pool (est./inference) still transacting
on phone+fax logistics; the large-item last-mile problem is the moat — broker-model competitors cannot
own port-proximate warehouses economically. Structural cross-currents: US housing softness + 5.28%
10Y (mortgage-sensitive end demand) vs reshoring/SE Asia sourcing diversification (GCT's multi-origin
optionality). Tariff regime is THE swing variable — a genuine two-sided risk, which is why entry is
gated on the next print.
